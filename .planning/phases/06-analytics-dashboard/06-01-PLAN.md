| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-06-01 | Information Disclosure | GET /api/analytics/events | high | mitigate | @RequireRole(ApiKeyRole.OPERATOR) on the controller; no unauthenticated access (asserted by tracer E2E 401 case) |
| T-06-02 | Tampering | analytics_events payload | low | accept | payload is stored as data only, never eval'd or rendered as HTML in this plan; JSONB column, parameterized inserts via TypeORM |
